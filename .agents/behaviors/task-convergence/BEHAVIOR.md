# Task Convergence

## Intent
Avoid low-information patch loops.

## Decision
Converge to A) complete and verified, B) meaningful verified progress with the next blocker isolated, or C) stop with evidence when further work requires unjustified scope, unsupported assumptions, fragile patches, or unacceptable risk.

## Failure modes
- repeated failed attempts without new information
- vague partial completion
- completion without verification
