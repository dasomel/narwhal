# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

English | [한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT-ko.md)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
* Respecting differing viewpoints and constructive technical discussions
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
* Focusing on the long-term reliability and health of the open source platform

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and impartially.
